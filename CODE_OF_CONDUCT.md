# Code of conduct

Remvyn is a small project and we want it to be a welcoming one, whoever you are and however much experience you have.

## Expected

- Be kind and assume good intent. Most misunderstandings are just that.
- Critique code and ideas, not people. Explain why, and suggest what would be better.
- Be patient with newcomers and with questions that have been asked before.
- Respect maintainers' time and decisions, including a "no" to a feature.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal or political attacks.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or content.
- Threats, or encouraging others to harass someone.
- Publishing someone's private information (addresses, emails, server details, keys) without their permission.
- Repeatedly pushing a point after you've been asked to stop.

## Scope

This applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when you represent the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour bothers you, contact the maintainer privately at rajvirahmedshuvo10@gmail.com. Reports are kept confidential.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, lock threads, and warn or ban anyone who breaks these rules, in proportion to what happened.
