# Remvyn — naming and identity

Research date: **24 October 2026**. Product brief verified against the repository's `README.md`. This is a design proposal; no application names, code, repository names, domains, accounts, or releases were changed.

**Round 2 status:** Remvyn and the lowercase lettering are approved. The symbol has been replaced by the routed R described below. The earlier naming research is retained as historical evidence; alternative names are no longer active.

## Recommendation

**Remvyn**, pronounced **REM-vin**. Six letters, two syllables, with a useful suggestion of “remote” and enough breadth for SSH, files, encrypted vault sync, and server visibility. The spelling is coined; it does not have an established dictionary meaning. Use **Remvyn** in prose and the supplied lowercase lettering in the logo. Suggested descriptive line: **SSH access. Servers in view.**

Remvyn has the cleanest observed results in this screen: no named software product identified in the target domain; zero GitHub repository-name results; no exact GitHub account or npm package returned; zero US Apple software-search results; no TMview contains-search rows; and not-found responses for all four requested domains. These are dated observations, not a guarantee of availability or legal clearance.

The principal tradeoff is spelling from speech: “Remvin” is a plausible transcription. A targeted phonetic-spelling web search found unrelated businesses and incidental text, with no relevant tool identified. Similar-sounding marks and international-language meanings have not been comprehensively cleared. [Remmina](https://remmina.org/) is an established remote desktop client; the shared “Rem” prefix is a residual similarity consideration, even though the names differ in spelling, syllables, and ending.

## Logo and palette

**Round 2 selection: routed R.** This is deliberately an **R monogram** for Remvyn. A full-height left spine, one closed upper bowl and one descending right leg establish that reading. The leg turns into a short, square-ended horizontal connection terminal, hinting at an SSH route without adding a separate cable, arrow, lock, prompt, status dot or lightning bolt. One filled compound path carries the complete symbol. The primary reading is R; connection is the secondary association.

Unlike the rejected paired-route mark, this silhouette has no opposing hooks or rotational symmetry. Its continuous left spine and closed counter prevent the S / 5 / Z / 2 / G / C readings observed or screened for in this round. The bowl stays open internally at 16px, the spine occupies two full pixels, and the diagonal leg remains separate from the left stem below the bowl. These are designer observations from rendered specimens, not results of a stranger/user study or a guarantee that every viewer will agree.

The **approved lowercase lettering is unchanged**: the complete `#EFF2FA` lettering group, every outline, all letter advances and its `translate(270 88)` placement are byte-identical to `archive/round1/wordmark.svg`. Only the symbol group in the lockup is replaced. No font is used by the final artwork; no raster is embedded or traced. The mark, desktop icon, Android foreground and wordmark are hand-authored clean vector SVGs. Accessible `<title>` elements describe the art; there are no visible `<text>` elements inside the icons.

## Usage and implementation notes

- `mark.svg`: transparent **1024 × 1024** symbol master; painted bounds **192..832** on each axis. Keep the aspect ratio and padding. Recolor the complete filled path together for monochrome variants. Native template behavior belongs to later application integration.
- `app-icon.svg`: **1024 × 1024**, unchanged inset rounded dark tile and subtle edge, new R at 90% of master scale. `app-icon-1024.png` has transparent outer corners. No text, gradient, glow or raster effects.
- `android-foreground.svg`: **108 × 108dp**, transparent and unmasked. The master is transformed by `translate(17.136 17.136) scale(.072)`, placing all artwork in **30.96..77.04dp** on both axes. Its furthest point/control hull is at most **32.584dp** from **(54,54)**, inside the **33dp radius / 66dp diameter** adaptive safe circle. `android-background.txt` remains **#15161A**. Do not bake the desktop tile into the foreground. Android XML/VectorDrawable conversion belongs to application integration.
- `wordmark.svg`: **1100 × 300**, same approved lowercase letter paths and spacing, new R symbol. Suggested minimum display width **160 CSS px**; use the standalone symbol below that. Preserve at least one stem thickness of external clear space beyond the intrinsic canvas.
- Use the supplied mark at **16px and above**. Do not add small decorative features, status colors or text inside the app icon. Real alert indicators belong outside the brand mark.
- All round-one symbol-bearing SVGs/PNGs, the old preview/generator/document and the Velqin alternative are preserved under `archive/round1/`. Current `alt-mark.svg` and `alt-mark-32.png` were removed after archiving.

## Rendering and verification

`generate.cjs` is the geometry and rendering source. It uses **@resvg/resvg-js 2.6.2**, with system-font scanning disabled; only the review sheet uses Arial for labels. Icon masters contain no font dependency. All delivered PNGs are rendered by resvg from the actual corresponding SVG files, with no raster retouching. The generator re-renders each current deliverable and checks exact PNG buffer equality, and verifies that the approved lettering group is byte-identical. Results and source/output SHA-256 hashes are recorded in `verification.json`.

Visual inspection covered all four concepts at **16, 24, 32 and 256px**, the final icon composited on **#111216**, actual-size final 16/24/32px PNGs with pixel enlargements, and the Android foreground through circle, rounded-square and square masks. The responsive `identity-preview.html` was rendered and inspected at **728px and 360px** widths. The final R counter, spine and leg remain distinguishable at 16px. All four current master SVGs parse successfully and contain no `<image>` or `<text>` elements. No application tests are needed for standalone branding artifacts.

Reproduce from the repository root:

```sh
npm install --prefix branding/.tools --cache branding/.npm-cache --no-audit --no-fund @resvg/resvg-js@2.6.2
node branding/generate.cjs
```

Renderer dependencies/cache are temporary and are removed after verification; rerunning the install recreates them inside `branding/`. The generator reads the approved lettering from `archive/round1/wordmark.svg`. Reference images are retained with source URLs and can be reassembled with `node branding/references/render.cjs` after installing the renderer. Neither the current generator nor reference renderer modifies the archive.

## Remaining uncertainty

- A no-result search is not proof of global name exclusivity. Private repositories, unindexed launches, local stores, unregistered rights, translation/transliteration and similar-sounding brands can be missed.
- Direct USPTO search was unavailable. TMview is EUIPO-maintained but is not an official legal register; participating-office data and update coverage can vary. No blanket US/EU legal clearance is claimed.
- Domain eligibility, premium/reserved status and purchase prices were not checked at registrar checkout. GitHub/npm claimability was not tested by creating accounts or publishing packages.
- Osvren's site purpose, Auvren's class-9 goods, and Nivrel's class-42 goods remain incompletely verified. These gaps are reasons to avoid or demote those names, not evidence that the names are clear.
- This proposal does not install icons, create `.icns` / `.ico` / Android XML resources, rename code, commit, push, or publish anything. Those remain outside this design-only task.
